Privacy Policy

Updated: August 15, 2026

Herna is a games application that combines multiple standalone games in one environment.

1. Local gameplay data

The app may store player profiles, settings, saved games, results, leaderboards, achievements and other data required for gameplay locally on the device.

2. Accounts and personal information

Local player profiles are not, by themselves, online accounts. If a particular release uses external Google Play services, relevant information will be included in that release's declarations.

3. Ads and purchases

Monetization features may vary between releases. Before a public release that uses ads or purchases, this policy will be updated to reflect the actual behaviour of that build.

4. Children

Herna may be used by different age groups. It is not designed to intentionally collect children's personal information.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, services or legal requirements change.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent using the contact details published with the relevant app on Google Play or its product listing.
