PixelAmp

PixelAmp is a modern music player inspired by legendary desktop audio players. It combines compact Winamp-style controls with a modern interface, Android Auto, widgets, an equalizer, visualizers and advanced local music library management.

Your music. Your player.

PixelAmp is designed for listeners who want real control over their local music library while keeping playback fast, clear and enjoyable.

Features

A classic music player reimagined for modern Android

Winamp Classic & modern skins

An interface inspired by classic music players, combined with modern themes including Liquid Glass, OLED and additional visual styles.

Android Auto

Browse your music library, playlists, albums, artists, folders, favorites and playback queue directly from Android Auto.

Liquid Glass widgets

Responsive home-screen widgets with album artwork, track information, timeline and direct playback controls.

Equalizer & sound

Built-in equalizer, sound presets, volume controls and additional tools for adjusting the listening experience.

Visualizer

Spectrum analyzer, oscilloscope and combined visualization modes with configurable detail and appearance.

Music library

Tracks, albums, artists, folders, playlists, favorites, recently played music and the current playback queue.
