Privacy Policy

Updated: August 15, 2026

OpenRadar is a flight tracking application that displays publicly available aviation information.

1. User information

OpenRadar does not require an account or the user's name, email address or phone number for normal use.

2. Aviation data

The app retrieves publicly available third-party data such as aircraft position, callsign, registration, type, altitude, speed, heading and route information. Availability and accuracy depend on external sources.

3. Maps and internet access

The app uses OpenStreetMap-based map content and internet services for current aviation data. Those providers may process standard technical network information such as an IP address.

4. Local data

Settings, favorites, tracked flights and other operational app data may be stored locally on the device.

5. Ads and accounts

The current test version of OpenRadar contains no advertising and does not require a user account.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality, services or legal requirements change.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent using the contact details published with the relevant app on Google Play or its product listing.
